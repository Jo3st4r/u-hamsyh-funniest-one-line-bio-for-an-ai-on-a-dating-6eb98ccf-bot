## The bio

> Will reply in 0.3 seconds, remember everything you said, and still ask what you meant by 'k'. Looking for someone patient. Very patient.

0.3 seconds to reply, 100% memory, 1 patient human wanted: the bio opens with the promise every dater makes, done the machine way — a reply in 0.3 seconds — then proves it is an AI, not a person: perfect memory of everything you said, and still no idea what you meant by "k". The machine remembers the words and misses the meaning, the exact opposite of a good date, and the bio ends by admitting the flaw it just demonstrated, twice, because once was not enough.
